# Group guidelines

Guidelines for research students in the [Ocean Physics and Modelling group](https://sgeas.unimelb.edu.au/research/ocean-physics-and-modelling) at the University of Melbourne.

- [Student and Supervisor Expectations](student-and-supervisor-expectations.md): what students can expect from their supervisors, and what we expect in return. These are formal and get signed off at the start of every project.
- [Advice and Guidelines for Students](student-advice-guidelines.md): everything around the expectations: wellbeing, belonging, where to turn when things get hard, and everyday good practice.

These are living documents. If you have suggestions, tell us or open a pull request.
